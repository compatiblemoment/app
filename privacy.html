Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Compatible Moment mobile application (the “Application”), operated by Oleksandr Pavliuchyk (the “Service Provider”).

Information the Application Collects

The Application does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to the Service Provider or to third-party analytics or advertising services. The Application does not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising trackers, or its own server to collect user information.

The Application does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use its features, unless this is expressly stated in a future version of the Application.

Subscriptions and In-App Purchases

The Application offers an optional auto-renewable monthly subscription that provides access to premium features. The subscription price is displayed in the Application and in the App Store before purchase. The current listed price is USD $0.99 per month; the actual price shown by Apple may vary by storefront, currency, taxes, or local pricing.

Purchases are handled using Apple’s StoreKit framework and Apple’s App Store payment system. The Application does not collect or receive your payment card details and does not send subscription information to a server operated by the Service Provider.

The Application uses StoreKit transaction and entitlement information available on the device to determine whether premium features should be enabled. Apple independently processes purchase-related information under its own terms and privacy policy. The Service Provider does not control Apple’s data processing practices.

The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it through your Apple Account subscription settings in accordance with Apple’s terms. Cancelling generally stops future renewals, while access to premium features normally continues until the end of the current paid period. Deleting or uninstalling the Application does not cancel an active subscription.

For questions about Apple’s handling of purchase information, billing, or refunds, consult Apple’s privacy information or contact Apple Support.

Third-Party Services

The Application does not use third-party analytics or advertising services and does not send user information to the Service Provider or other third parties. Apple’s App Store and StoreKit services are used to process in-app purchases and provide transaction information necessary for subscription functionality. Apple processes information according to its own policies and terms.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because the Application does not collect or store personal information on systems operated by the Service Provider, the Service Provider has no such Application-collected personal information to retain or delete. Apple may retain purchase, transaction, and Apple Account records under its own policies and legal obligations.
